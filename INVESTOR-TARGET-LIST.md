## 📋 HOW TO RESEARCH EACH INVESTOR

**For every investor, find:**

1. **Decision Maker**
   - Name
   - Title
   - Email (not generic)
   - Twitter/LinkedIn

2. **Fund Details**
   - Check size ($5M-$100M+)
   - Past investments (compatible?)
   - Location (will they travel to you?)
   - Investment thesis (mission-aligned?)

3. **Contact Method**
   - Direct email: best
   - LinkedIn: second best
   - Twitter DM: third
   - Cold email: last resort

4. **Why They'd Fund You**
   - Their thesis
   - Their portfolio
   - Their history
   - What they value

---

## 🎯 OUTREACH STRATEGY

**Priority Order:**

1. **Warm Intros First** (50 asks to friends/mentors)
   - Ask: "Do you know anyone at [Fund]?"
   - Win rate: 30-50%

2. **Twitter/LinkedIn Engagement** (Week 1-2)
   - Follow them
   - Like their posts
   - Comment thoughtfully
   - DM if they engage

3. **Cold Emails** (Week 2-3)
   - Personalized (not mass)
   - Reference their thesis
   - Show you know their work
   - 20% win rate if good

4. **Product Hunt Launch** (Week 3)
   - Launch NyxScream
   - Get 1K+ upvotes
   - Investors see traction
   - This is GOLD for credibility

5. **Pitch Events** (Week 3-4)
   - Startup competitions
   - Demo days
   - Pitch nights
   - Networking events

6. **Follow-up Sequence** (Ongoing)
   - Day 3: First follow-up
   - Day 7: Second follow-up
   - Day 14: Third follow-up
   - Day 21: Move on or pivot
