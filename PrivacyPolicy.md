# NYXSCREAM - PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** August 2, 2026

## **1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT**

### **Account Information:**
- Email address
- Display name / Username
- Profile photo
- Bio / Description
- Payment information (via Stripe/IAP)

### **Usage Data:**
- Watch history
- Search queries
- Streaming activity
- Engagement (likes, comments)
- Device information
- IP address & geolocation
- Browser/app version

### **Creator Data:**
- Video uploads & metadata
- Stream analytics
- Earnings & payouts
- Creator verification documents

### **Communication:**
- Chat messages
- Support tickets
- Feedback & surveys

## **2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA**

✅ Provide and improve the Platform
✅ Personalize your experience
✅ Send notifications (if opted-in)
✅ Process payments
✅ Content moderation & safety
✅ Analytics & research
✅ Legal compliance
✅ Fraud prevention

---

## **3. DATA SHARING**

We share data with:
- **Service Providers:** Firebase, Stripe, Mux, AWS
- **Moderators:** For content review
- **Payment Processors:** For transactions
- **Legal Authorities:** If required by law
- **Creators:** Public stats (not personal data)

**We DO NOT sell your personal data to third parties.**

---

## **4. DATA RETENTION**

- **Account Data:** Kept while active + 90 days after deletion
- **Chat Messages:** Kept for 1 year
- **Analytics:** Kept for 2 years
- **Payment Records:** Kept for 7 years (legal requirement)
- **Deleted Content:** Removed within 30 days
