## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### DO:
✅ Personalize each email (mention their past investments)
✅ Lead with mission (not just numbers)
✅ Be authentic (solo founder energy)
✅ Keep short (under 200 words for initial)
✅ Include demo link (show, don't just tell)
✅ Follow up exactly 5 days later (not sooner)
✅ Respect their time (no pushy follow-ups)
✅ Make ask clear (15 min call? Demo?)

### DON'T:
❌ Mass email (personalize every one)
❌ Lead with financial projections (lead with mission)
❌ Make it salesy (be authentic)
❌ Send long emails (they won't read)
❌ Send without demo/deck links (back up claims)
❌ Follow up before 5 days (annoying)
❌ Be pushy (respect their decision)
❌ Make ask vague (what exactly do you want?)
