## 🎯 THE FOLLOW-UP FRAMEWORK

**Rule 1:** If they don't respond in 3 days, follow up
**Rule 2:** Never ask "Did you get my email?" (they did)
**Rule 3:** Always give them a new reason to engage
**Rule 4:** Track everything (who you've reached out to, when, response)
**Rule 5:** After 3 follow-ups with no response = move on

**When to use:** After 2+ serious investors show interest
**Caution:** Only use if true

---

### **Tactic 3: The "Founder Update"**

Every 2 weeks, email all interested investors:

"Here's what we shipped this week:
- [Feature 1]
- [Metric 1]
- [Update 1]

How can we help if you're considering investing?"

**When to use:** Keep them warm between meetings

---

### **Tactic 4: The "Ask for Advice" Follow-up**

"I'm getting feedback that [concern]. What's your take on this?"

**Why:** Gives them a reason to engage that's not about money

---

### **Tactic 5: The "Partner Introduction" Follow-up**

"I'm talking to [relevant company] about a partnership. Thought you'd want to know since it relates to our thesis."

**Why:** Shows momentum without explicitly asking for money

## ❌ FOLLOW-UP MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Mistake 1: Same message every time**
"Did you get my email?" = Delete
Use new angle each time

❌ **Mistake 2: Waiting too long**
"I'll wait a week" = Too late
Follow up Day 3

❌ **Mistake 3: Following up too much**
10+ emails = Spam
Max 4 before moving on

❌ **Mistake 4: Sounding desperate**
"Really hope you respond" = Red flag
Sound confident always

❌ **Mistake 5: Negative follow-up**
"I guess you're not interested" = Petty
Stay positive always

---

## ✅ BEST PRACTICES

✅ Every follow-up has a new angle
✅ Always give them a reason to respond
✅ Keep messages short (2-3 paragraphs)
✅ Make next step crystal clear
✅ Track everything in spreadsheet
✅ Respect their time (max 4 follow-ups)
✅ Move on after Day 21 with grace
✅ Revisit with traction in 6 months
