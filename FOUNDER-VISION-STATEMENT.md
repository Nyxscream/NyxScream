One core feature sets NyxScream apart: the **Community Giving Ledger**.

But here's the difference:

**You can SEE it happen in real-time.**

Not a "donate" button that disappears into darkness. A LEDGER. A DATABASE. A RECORD OF IMPACT.

Every creator knows exactly how much their community has given. Every viewer sees their contribution MATTER. Every philanthropic act is DOCUMENTED and CELEBRATED.

This is not charity. This is COMMUNITY.
