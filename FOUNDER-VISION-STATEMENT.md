# Founder Vision Statement - Isaac Solomon Robert

**NyxScream Will Change How Creators Are Treated**

---

## WHO I AM

My name is Isaac Solomon Robert. I'm a Nigerian web developer and welder who taught myself to code. I'm building NyxScream because I see a problem that breaks my heart: creators are being exploited.

I'm not building this for money. I'm building this for mission.

---

## WHAT I SEE

I see 10M+ creators globally who have no voice.

- Less privileged people can't access tools
- Handicapped creators can't use mainstream platforms
- Global South creators are invisible to global audience
- Dark content creators are ignored by mainstream

And meanwhile, Twitch is taking 50% of their earnings. YouTube is exploiting their data. Meta is trying to own their audience.

It's wrong. And I'm going to fix it.

---

## WHY I'M BUILDING NYXSCREAM

I'm building NyxScream because creators deserve better.

**Creators deserve:**
- Fair revenue share (80%+, not 50%)
- Privacy (not data exploitation)
- Community (not algorithmic loneliness)
- Voice (not algorithmic silencing)
- Opportunity (not invisible platforms)
- Respect (not exploitation)

NyxScream provides all of this.

**I will NEVER sell NyxScream. Ever.**

Not for $100M. Not for $1B. Not for any price.

Why?

Because if I sell to Meta, Google, or Amazon, they will:
- Add ads everywhere (betray creators)
- Exploit creator data (betray privacy)
- Algorithmic control (betray autonomy)
- Remove from dark content focus (betray mission)
- Monetize creator relationships (betray trust)

I'd rather die building NyxScream than live knowing I sold creators back into exploitation.

This company exists forever. It serves creators forever. The mission never changes.

## HOW I THINK ABOUT MONEY

Money matters. But not in the way most founders think.

**Money is a tool, not a goal.**

I'm not raising $2M to get rich. I'm not building this to exit for $1B and retire.

I'm raising $2M because:
- I built this alone on my phone
- I need infrastructure to scale
- I need team to support creators
- I need runway to hit profitability
- I need capital to serve 10M+ people

That's it. Money enables mission, not the other way around.

**By Year 3, we're profitable with $67M annual profit.** We don't need more capital. We don't need investors' money anymore. We self-fund growth from profits.

The investor who backs me at $2M isn't giving me capital I need forever. They're giving me capital I need temporarily. After Year 2, we don't need them anymore (for capital). We only need them as advisors.

This is intentional. I don't want to be dependent on capital raising. I don't want VCs breathing down my neck. I don't want quarterly earnings pressure.

I want mission autonomy. This business model gives it to me.

## WHY I BUILT THIS SOLO

I could have raised capital earlier. I could have hired a co-founder. I could have built faster.

But I chose to build alone. Why?

Because I wanted to prove something:

**The idea works without capital.**
**The business model is sound.**
**The vision is clear.**
**The founder is committed.**

When I ask investors for money, I'm not asking for validation of an idea. I'm asking for capital to scale what I've already proven works.

This is powerful. This changes the conversation.

Investor isn't taking a risk on an unproven founder with an unproven idea. Investor is funding a founder who's already executed and a product that already works.

This reduces their risk. This makes me less dependent on them. This gives me leverage in negotiations.

I did this intentionally.

Some investors will suggest installing a professional CEO. 

I will not allow this.

Why?

Because CEOs are replaceable. Mission isn't.

The mission is bigger than me. The mission is bigger than any CEO.

The mission is eternal.
