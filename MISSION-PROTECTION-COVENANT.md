**This mission is sacred. This covenant protects it.**

## NON-NEGOTIABLE COMMITMENTS

### 1. NEVER SELL

NyxScream will NEVER be acquired, merged, or sold to:
- Tech giants (Meta, Google, Amazon, Apple)
- Private equity firms
- Competitors
- Anyone, for any price

**This is permanent.** No amount of money changes this.

**Exception:** Only if founder voluntarily chooses to liquidate for personal reasons, AND mission is transferred to independent nonprofit that continues work.

---

### 2. CREATOR REVENUE SHARE

Creators will ALWAYS keep ≥80% of revenue generated.

**Current model:**
- Creator: 80%
- Philanthropy Ledger: 10%
- Operations: 10%

**Can only change by:**
- Increasing creator share (to 85%+, etc)
- NEVER decreasing creator share

**Locked in:** This ratio is permanent and binding.

---

### 3. PHILANTHROPY LEDGER

Minimum 10% of revenue goes to helping less privileged people.

**Uses:**
- Direct cash assistance to less privileged creators
- Fund technology access programs
- Support creators without resources
- Emergency assistance for vulnerable creators
- Community development projects

**Cannot decrease:** Percentage locked at ≥10%.

**Locked in:** This commitment survives founder (becomes endowment if needed).

---

### 4. MISSION FOCUS: DARK CONTENT

NyxScream remains dark-themed horror/thriller streaming platform.

**This means:**
- Content focus stays on dark, mature themes
- Horror, thriller, psychological content prioritized
- Creators can stream any mature content safely
- Platform culture stays dark/edgy/authentic
- Won't pivot to mainstream "family-friendly"

**Why:** This differentiates us AND serves underserved audience.

**Locked in:** Mission scope cannot change without founder approval.

---

### 5. CREATOR-FIRST VALUES

Every decision must prioritize creators first.

**Examples of creator-first:**
✅ Revenue share over platform profit
✅ Creator safety over advertiser requirements
✅ Creator autonomy over algorithmic control
✅ Creator voice over investor directives
✅ Creator privacy over data monetization

**Examples of betrayal (never happening):**
❌ Adding ads to creator streams
❌ Exploiting creator data
❌ Reducing revenue share
❌ Imposing content restrictions
❌ Selling creator information

**Locked in:** Creator-first is non-negotiable.

---

Isaac Solomon Robert retains these powers forever:

**CEO Authority:**
- Sole authority on product decisions
- Sole authority on creator policies
- Sole authority on major hires
- Sole authority on strategic direction

**Board Veto:**

**Locked in:** Founder control is permanent (or transferred to chosen successor).

NyxScream will remain independent forever.

**This means:**
- No acquisition (under any circumstance)
- No merger (with anyone)
- No sale (to anyone)
- No IPO (that would enable acquisition)
- No pivot to exit-friendly model

**Exception:** Only if company is failing AND founder voluntarily chooses to transfer assets to mission-aligned nonprofit. Even then, the nonprofit continues independence.

**Locked in:** Independence is permanent.

Investors commit to:

### If Mission is Threatened

**Founder has power to:**
1. Reject investor proposal (if mission-threatening)
2. Remove investor from board (if mission-threatening)
3. Forced buyback of investor stake (if necessary)
4. Replace investor with mission-aligned partner
5. Take company private (if needed)
6. Transfer mission to nonprofit (nuclear option)

**Investor cannot:**
- Overrule founder on mission
- Replace founder with new CEO
- Force acquisition/exit
- Remove founder's veto power
- Change terms of this covenant

### If Founder Violates

**Investors can:**
- Call emergency board meeting
- Demand mission restoration
- Legal action to enforce covenant
- Force founder buyback option
- Transfer company to mission-aligned nonprofit

**But investor cannot:**
- Replace founder unilaterally
- Change mission themselves
- Pivot to acquisition strategy
- Reduce mission protections

## DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**If founder and investors disagree on mission:**

1. **Discussion:** Meet to understand disagreement
2. **Arbitration:** Bring in neutral mission expert (not investor)
3. **Mediation:** Neutral party helps find resolution
4. **Decision:** Founder makes final call on mission (investor has recourse option below)
5. **Recourse:** If investor fundamentally disagrees, they can:
   - Request forced buyback at fair valuation
   - Sell stake to mission-aligned investor
   - Accept founder's decision
