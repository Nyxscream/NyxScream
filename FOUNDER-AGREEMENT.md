## 2. FOUNDER OWNERSHIP & VESTING

### 2.1 Founder Shares

Founder shall own **1,000,000 (One Million) founder shares** representing 100% ownership of the Company prior to any external investment.

### 2.2 Vesting Schedule

Founder's shares are subject to the following vesting schedule:

**4-Year Vesting with 1-Year Cliff:**

Year 1: 25% vested (250,000 shares)
Year 2: 50% vested (500,000 shares)
Year 3: 75% vested (750,000 shares)
Year 4: 100% vested (1,000,000 shares)

**Cliff Period:** Founder must remain with the Company for 12 months before any shares vest. If Founder leaves before 12 months, all shares remain unvested and revert to the Company.

**Continued Vesting:** After the 1-year cliff, remaining shares vest monthly over the next 3 years at a rate of approximately 20,833 shares per month.

**Full Vesting:** All shares are fully vested after 4 years of continuous service with the Company.

### 2.3 Purpose of Vesting Schedule

This vesting schedule demonstrates Founder's long-term commitment to building NyxScream. The schedule ensures that: Founder is incentivized to remain with the Company through critical growth phases, Investors are protected against key-person risk (founder leaving immediately after funding), Founder's equity grows as the Company grows, All stakeholders (Founder, investors, employees) are aligned on long-term success.

## 6. TERMINATION & ACCELERATION

### 6.1 Termination by Company

If the Company terminates Founder's employment without cause before full vesting: Founder shall keep all vested shares, All unvested shares revert to the Company.

### 6.2 Termination by Founder

If Founder voluntarily leaves the Company before full vesting: Founder shall keep all vested shares, All unvested shares revert to the Company.

### 6.3 Single Trigger Acceleration

In the event of a **Change of Control** (acquisition, merger, sale of >50% of assets): All unvested shares automatically accelerate and become fully vested, Founder receives full value of all shares upon acquisition.

### 6.4 Double Trigger Acceleration

If, following a Change of Control, Founder is terminated without cause within 12 months: All remaining unvested shares accelerate.
