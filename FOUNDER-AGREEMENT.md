## SECTION 2: VESTING SCHEDULE

### 2.1 Founder Vesting

**Important Note:** Isaac has already performed 100% of work (built entire company solo). Vesting is NOT about earning equity for future work, but rather showing commitment to mission and company longevity.

**Vesting Schedule:** 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff

**Breakdown:**
- Year 0-1: 0% vested (cliff period = 1 year)
- Year 1: 25% vested (250,000 shares)
- Year 2: 50% vested (500,000 shares)
- Year 3: 75% vested (750,000 shares)
- Year 4: 100% vested (1,000,000 shares)

**Vesting Rate:** 208,333 shares per quarter

**Monthly Vesting:** 16,667 shares per month

### 2.2 Acceleration Events

**Double-Trigger Acceleration:**
- If founder is terminated without cause: Remaining unvested shares vest immediately
- If company is acquired without founder approval: Remaining unvested shares vest immediately
- Note: Company will NEVER be acquired, so this is largely symbolic

**Single-Trigger Acceleration:**
- Founder can request emergency acceleration if health crisis
- Board (investor) approval required for single-trigger

### 2.3 Departure & Forfeiture

**If Founder Voluntarily Departs:**
- Remaining unvested shares forfeit
- Vested shares remain with founder
- Founder keeps title "Founder" forever

**If Founder Terminated for Cause:**
- Cause defined narrowly: Criminal conduct, gross negligence
- Remaining unvested shares forfeit
- Vested shares remain with founder

**If Founder Terminated Without Cause:**
- Remaining unvested shares vest immediately
- All vested shares + accelerated shares remain with founder
- Severance negotiable

## SECTION 6: MISSION PROTECTION

### 6.1 Mission Lock-In

**NyxScream's mission is sacred and protected forever.**

**Non-negotiable mission elements:**
1. Creators keep 80%+ revenue (never reduced)
2. Philanthropy Ledger 10% (never reduced)
3. Dark content focus (never pivoted)
4. Creator-first values (never compromised)
5. Company independence (never sold)
6. Founder CEO control (never replaced)

### 6.2 Founder Veto Power

**Founder has absolute veto over mission decisions.**

If investor or board proposes any change to:
- Creator revenue share (founder vetoes)
- Philanthropy commitment (founder vetoes)
- Content focus (founder vetoes)
- Independence goal (founder vetoes)
- Creator policies (founder vetoes)

**Founder decision is final. Investor cannot override.**

### 6.3 Mission Protection - Investor Removal

**If investor threatens mission, founder can:**
1. Remove investor from board (requires founder majority, which founder has as founder + proxy)
2. Force buyback of investor equity (at fair valuation)
3. Replace investor with mission-aligned partner
4. Take company private (if needed)

**Process:**
- Founder identifies mission threat
- Founder calls emergency board meeting
- Founder explains threat
- Investor gets 30 days to reverse position
- If investor refuses, forced buyback process begins

**Forced Buyback Terms:**
- Investor gets return of capital + 20% annual return (whichever is lower)
- Paid from company reserves over 3 years
- Investor loses board seat immediately
- Company remains independent

### 6.4 Acquisition Block

**This company will NEVER be acquired or sold, regardless of offer.**

**Founder has absolute veto over any:**
- Acquisition (blocked)
- Merger (blocked)
- Sale (blocked)
- Strategic partnership that risks mission (blocked)

Even if investor wants to sell, founder can:
- Veto acquisition completely
- Force company to remain independent
- Buy out investor (if needed)
- Transfer mission to nonprofit (nuclear option)
