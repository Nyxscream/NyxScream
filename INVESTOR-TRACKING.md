## Email Status Legend

- **PENDING** = Not sent yet
- **DRAFT** = Email written, ready to send
- **SENT** = Email sent, waiting for response
- **BOUNCED** = Email bounced back
- **OPENED** = Recipient opened email (if trackable)

---

## Response Status Legend

- **No** = No response yet
- **Auto Reply** = Got automatic response
- **Yes - Interested** = Positive response
- **Yes - Pass** = Declined but polite
- **Yes - Needs Info** = Asked for more information
- **No Response (7 days)** = Time to follow up

---

## Meeting Status Legend

- **No** = Meeting not scheduled
- **Scheduled** = Meeting on calendar
- **Completed** = Meeting happened
- **Rescheduled** = New date set
- **Cancelled** = They cancelled

---

## Current Status Legend

- **QUEUE** = Ready to send email
- **ACTIVE** = Email sent, waiting response
- **INTERESTED** = Positive response, exploring
- **MEETING** = Meeting scheduled/happened
- **NEGOTIATING** = Term sheet discussion
- **CLOSE** = Near final deal
- **DONE** = Deal signed
- **PASS** = Not a fit, moving on
- **RESEARCH** = Still researching investor
